# Demo Images Sources & Ground-Truth Reference

This document catalogs every image in `demo-images/` (22 images total: 8 original prototype fixtures, 8 real textbook graphs from OpenStax, and 6 synthetic/camera-perspective test images), including exact provenance, licensing, coordinate axis bounds, and ground-truth mathematical content.

> **Important Note:** None of the filenames, source descriptions, or reference equations documented here are ever passed to `detector.py` or `reader.py` at runtime; image recognition operates strictly on raw pixel data and local OCR.
